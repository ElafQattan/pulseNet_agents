# INC-2024-09: roadworks cut a ring segment and a site spur in the same duct

**What happened.** An excavator cut duct D-077. The ring segment and the fiber spur to a cell site ran in the same
duct and were cut together. The ring protection worked, but the site was down for 7 hours.

**What slowed us down.** Two separate alarms were handled by two engineers as two problems. Nobody connected them
for 40 minutes.

**Lessons.** Alarms in the same minute in the same duct are one incident (shared-risk link group). Ticket the fiber
team once with both cuts. Protect the neighbour sites early.
