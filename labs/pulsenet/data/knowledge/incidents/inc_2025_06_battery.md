# INC-2025-06: cell site ran out of battery during a heatwave power cut

**What happened.** A utility fault cut mains power. The generator arrived after 2 h 40 min; the batteries lasted
2 h 10 min. The site was down for 30 minutes.

**Lesson.** Enable energy saving as soon as fewer than 120 minutes are left: it would have tripled the battery time.
Ask for the generator immediately, not when the battery is low.
