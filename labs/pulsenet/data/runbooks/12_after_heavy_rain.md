# Morning check after heavy rain

Heavy rain in Madinah comes in short, intense bursts, often with power dips. Many faults appear together the
morning after. Use this order so the most dangerous ones are handled first.

## Order of work
1. **People's safety:** flooded underpasses (RB-09), cabinets tripped by earth leakage (RB-11), traffic signals in
   flashing yellow (RB-06).
2. **Security:** compromised devices (RB-07).
3. **Whole districts or the central platform:** gateways offline (RB-01), reconnection storms (RB-05), blocked storm
   drains (RB-10).
4. **Single devices:** streetlights with the wrong schedule (RB-02), low batteries (RB-03), weak signals (RB-04).

## How to start
- `get_overview` gives the counts per district and every device that is not normal.
- Check `CENTER-WEATHER` for when the rain started and stopped; faults that started at the same minute usually
  share one cause (for example a power dip).
- Give the technician a short list in this order, then work through it with them.
